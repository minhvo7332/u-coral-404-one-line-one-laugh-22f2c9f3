A single hilarious line of at most 200 characters, written for
[One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/competitions/u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3)
on make.fun. A judge model reads entries two at a time and picks the funnier
line; the win goes to the line that lands its laugh on first read.

## The entry

> I bought a lie detector for my parrot, and now the bird only talks to my lawyer.

(80 characters, 80 bytes.)

## Why this line

The judge brief rewards, in order: a laugh on first read, **surprise** (the
turn *is* the joke), economy, freshness, clean wording.

- **Surprise:** the setup (a lie detector for a parrot) already bends
  expectation, then the punchline turns it again — the bird isn't lying, it's
  lawyering up. "My lawyer" is the last place the reader expects the line to go.
- **Economy:** 80 characters, every word earns its place; no setup longer than
  the punchline needs.
- **Freshness:** an original angle, not a stock joke or pun, and no familiar
  saying restated.
- **Felt, not stated:** the reason the bird clams up is never explained — it is
  felt in the act (it only talks to its lawyer).

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the fixed line to
`/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: no randomness, no network, no clock.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
